10 Random Pins

Pick a country. We drop 10 pins where people live.

    …or click any country on the map.

    How are pins chosen?
    • Major metros (metro pop. 400k+) get most pins, mostly in and near downtown rather than the suburbs.
    • Towns within 50 miles of those metros come next.
    • Towns within 50 miles of landmarks & tourist spots (UNESCO sites, national parks, roadside icons).
    • Small countries and states (under ~1M people) are fully eligible.
    • Every pin snaps to official Google Street View imagery near a town, so you see streets and buildings, not empty fields.
